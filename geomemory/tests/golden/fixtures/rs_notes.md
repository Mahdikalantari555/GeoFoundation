# Remote Sensing Notes

Remote sensing collects information about Earth without physical contact.

## Sentinel-2 NDVI

NDVI measures vegetation health from red and near-infrared reflectance.
Values range from -1 to 1; healthy vegetation is positive.

## Flood Detection Vector

Sentinel-1 SAR amplifies the water signal. Flood extent is masked where
backscatter drops sharply across a baseline.

## Change Detection

Time-series differencing of NDVI over two epochs highlights loss hotspots.
